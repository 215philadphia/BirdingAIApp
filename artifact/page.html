Birding AI

Choose a recording of a bird call and BirdNET names the species. It runs on this device, so your audio is never uploaded.

  1. On iPhone, record the bird in Voice Memos.
  2. Tap the recording's ••• menu, then Save to Files.
  3. Come back here and choose it. 10 to 60 seconds works best.

Choosing a place limits results to birds BirdNET expects there at that time of year, which makes it more accurate.
